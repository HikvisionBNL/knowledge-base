For common issues, such as no video preview during a call, we recommend first upgrading the indoor station and door station firmware to the latest versions available on the official website, and then adding the devices again through the wizard. This usually resolves most common issues.

If the issue persists, please provide:

1. Device models, firmware versions, and serial numbers
2. A detailed description of the issue, how often it occurs, and the system topology
3. Configuration screenshots and a video showing the issue
4. Troubleshooting steps already performed, such as restarting, resetting, upgrading, or changing the network
